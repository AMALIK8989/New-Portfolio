HisabFlow: one app for field-service and garage management

A business app for field-service companies and automotive garages. It keeps customers, jobs, vehicles, payments, expenses and the team in one place.

Overview

HisabFlow is a web app for field-service businesses and automotive garages. It brings customers, jobs, vehicles, payments, expenses and team members into one place, so day-to-day records don't have to be split across notebooks, spreadsheets and chat messages.

Screenshots

Who it is for

  • Field-service businesses that send teams out to customers' sites.
  • Automotive garages that track vehicles, repair jobs and payments.

What it covers

  • Customers and their contact details.
  • Jobs and the work done on each one.
  • Vehicles for garage work.
  • Payments and expenses.
  • Team members who take part in the work.

Platform

HisabFlow is built on Base44, an app-building platform, and is live at hisaab-flow.base44.app.
