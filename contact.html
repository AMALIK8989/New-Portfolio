Questions

Before you get in touch.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the project, any existing site or designs, and a rough sense of your budget and timeline. If you don't have these yet, that's fine. We'll work them out on a first call.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on the scope. You will get an estimated timeline in the written scope before any build starts.

Do you work on existing websites?

Yes. Many projects are redesigns, fixes or speed and SEO improvements on sites that already exist. I'll review what's there before suggesting changes.

Do you use AI tools?

Yes, I use AI tools such as Claude Code to speed up front-end work. Every project is reviewed, tested and owned by me, and backend logic and architecture are written by hand.

Who owns the code and the site after launch?

You do. You receive the code, a short handover guide for your team, and clear notes on how to make changes. Payment and ownership terms are agreed in writing before work starts.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Fixes and questions after go-live can be handled on an agreed basis, and I'm happy to discuss a monthly support arrangement if you'd like one.
