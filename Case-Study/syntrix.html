Syntrix Consultancy: clear services for business buyers

The website for my own consultancy. It explains web, WordPress and AI-driven software services in plain language, and presents two finished products.

Client
Syntrix Consultancy Services (my own brand)
My role
Design and development
Stack
HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5.3, AOS, Swiper
  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • Bootstrap 5.3
  • AOS
  • Swiper
  • JavaScript

Overview

Syntrix Consultancy Services offers web development, WordPress work, and AI and no-code software development. The website is the front door for those services and for two products built in-house.

Screenshots

The challenge

Business buyers need to understand what a consultancy can do for them within seconds. The site had to explain both services and finished products without turning into a catalogue.

Approach

I built the site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Bootstrap 5.3, with a dark teal colour system and a type pairing of Space Grotesk and Inter. AOS adds gentle reveals, and Swiper handles sliders.

Solution

  • Service pages for web development, WordPress, and AI and no-code software development.
  • OrderFlow, a point-of-sale and kitchen display system.
  • EduCore, a learning management system for schools.
