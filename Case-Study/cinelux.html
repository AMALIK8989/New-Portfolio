A premium streaming brand rebuilt from the earlier FlickRift site. It has a gold-accented design system, reusable page templates, and structured data on every page.

Overview

Cinelux is a premium streaming platform rebuilt from the earlier FlickRift free streaming site. The site is fully static, with no backend.

Screenshots

The challenge

Turn a free streaming site into a premium brand, with a consistent design and pages that search engines can read and rank.

Approach

I built a gold-accented design system and reusable page templates for movies and TV shows, so every new title follows the same proven structure.

Solution

  • Movie pages have a hero, player, synopsis, cast, recommendations and an FAQ.
  • Multi-season TV pages use a season selector.
  • Structured data on every page: Movie, TV, breadcrumbs and FAQ.
  • Metadata: Open Graph and Twitter tags, and hreflang tags for 13 languages.
