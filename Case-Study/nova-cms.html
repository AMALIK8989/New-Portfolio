NOVA CMS: managing a large media catalogue

A content management system for TV shows, movies and related media. It covers seasons, episodes and cast, with SEO controls and an image pipeline that serves responsive WebP files.

Overview

NOVA CMS manages a catalogue of TV shows, movies, cast and galleries. It is under active development, and the backend and admin features are the focus of my work on it.

Screenshots

The challenge

A large catalogue needs careful data handling, strong SEO controls, and images that load quickly on every device.

Approach

A Node.js and Express backend serves the admin tools, with Supabase as the database. Images go through a Sharp pipeline and are uploaded to Cloudinary, which produces WebP files at several breakpoints for responsive srcset delivery.

Solution

  • TV management covers seasons and episodes, with cast linked through Supabase joins.
  • Movie management is in place, with cast handling fixed for key-coercion bugs.
  • SEO management is a dedicated page wired to REST endpoints that support soft delete, restore and permanent delete.
  • A data fix: a Supabase row limit that was cutting off the actors table was traced to the PostgREST Max Rows setting and corrected.
